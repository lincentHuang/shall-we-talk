# 特別版卡牌美術提示詞

用 fal 的 `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit`（aspect_ratio 2:3、resolution 2K），以經典版的卡面 `assets/cards/love.jpg` 或卡背 `assets/cards/back.jpg` 當版型參考圖，讓新圖的框線與留白位置和經典版一致。

目前狀態：

- 已生成：深夜限定卡背（ad_back_a）、情話問答（ad_talk_b）、撩撥任務（ad_talk_a）
- 暫代（fal 餘額不足時用 tools/compose.py 合成）：祕密幻想、爸媽聊聊卡背與四種卡面

生成後把原圖放進這個資料夾，改 `tools/compose.py` 的 EXPORT 來源，再執行 `python3 art-source/editions/tools/compose.py`。

## ad_back（參考圖：卡背）

Keep the Art Nouveau / Alphonse Mucha illustration style with fine gold linework. Recolor everything to a deep midnight palette: inky indigo and navy blue, deep wine-burgundy and plum, with warm gold linework and accents. Inside the arch: a dark starry night sky with a large glowing golden crescent moon instead of the sun; the two elegant hands reaching toward each other now gently hold a single deep red rose, a flowing burgundy silk ribbon wraps around their wrists. Replace the pastel roses with dark red and burgundy roses, add a few small candle flames on the pillars, scattered golden stars and soft smoke-like mist. Sensual, romantic, intimate, mysterious, elegant. No text, no letters, no people other than the two hands. Flat illustration, crisp, high detail.
